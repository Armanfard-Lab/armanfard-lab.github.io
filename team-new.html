Principal Investigator

Dr. Narges Armanfard

Associate Professor (Tenured)

Dr. Narges Armanfard holds the position of Associate Professor (tenured) in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at McGill University, as well as at Mila - Quebec AI Institute. She is also affiliated with McGill Centre for Intelligent Machines (CIM), where she contributes to cutting-edge research in artificial intelligence and machine learning.
