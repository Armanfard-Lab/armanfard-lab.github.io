Research Areas

Theoretical AI

Foundational research in attention mechanisms, dimensionality reduction, time series analysis, and computer vision. We explore various learning paradigms including unsupervised learning, clustering, and anomaly detection.

AI in Industry

AI solutions for automation, predictive maintenance, and process optimization in autonomous driving and manufacturing. We develop intelligent systems that enhance efficiency and safety.

AI for Healthcare

Revolutionary AI technologies for healthcare, focusing on diagnostic accuracy, treatment optimization, and patient outcomes. Our algorithms support early disease detection and personalized medicine.

Collaboration Opportunities

We welcome collaborations with academic institutions, industry partners, and healthcare organizations. Our lab is open to research partnerships, student exchanges, and joint projects in artificial intelligence and machine learning.

If you are interested in collaborating with us or have questions about our research, please don't hesitate to reach out.
