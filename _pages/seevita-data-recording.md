### Data Recording Overview

SeeVita uses computer vision and multimodal signal processing to estimate vital signs from standard cameras, contact‑free. This page explains how to record high‑quality data safely and responsibly.

### How to Record

- **Camera**: Use a stable camera (laptop/desktop/phone) at 720p or higher.
- **Lighting**: Ensure your face is evenly lit. Avoid backlight or strong shadows.
- **Position**: Keep the camera at eye level; center your face in the frame.
- **Distance**: Sit ~40–80 cm from the camera (arm’s length).
- **Stillness**: Minimize movement and talking while recording; breathe normally.
- **Visibility**: Remove obstructions (e.g., masks) and keep hair away from the face.
- **Duration**: Record for 30–60 seconds for stable signals.
- **Device & Browser**: Use a modern browser and allow camera permissions.

### Privacy & Consent

- **Consent**: Participate only if you understand and agree to the purpose of recording. For research or clinical use, follow your local consent procedures.
- **Data Handling**: We prioritize privacy with minimal retention and de‑identification where feasible. Do not share sensitive content.
- **Usage**: Your data should only be used for the stated purpose. For enterprise integrations, a data processing agreement may be required.

If you have questions about privacy or usage, please use our <a href="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/contact/">Contact</a> page.

### Troubleshooting Tips

- Reduce screen brightness if it causes glare on your face.
- Close other apps using the camera.
- Improve lighting (a desk lamp facing you can help) and avoid backlit windows.
- Keep a neutral expression and steady posture during capture.
