## Workshops on Automated Spatial and Temporal Anomaly Detection (ASTAD)

ASTAD brings together researchers and practitioners working on AI methods for detecting, predicting, explaining, and responding to anomalies, abnormalities, outliers, risks, failures, and deviations from expected or learned normal behavior across spatial, temporal, and multimodal data. The workshop series is organized by iSMART Lab.
