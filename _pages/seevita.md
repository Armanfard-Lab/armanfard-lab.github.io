### What is SeeVita?

SeeVita is an iSMART Lab initiative focusing on intelligent visual assessment and analytics for healthcare and wellbeing. This page provides a concise subset of the full SeeVita website: a short overview, highlights of the team, and selected FAQs.

### Highlights

- <strong>Focus</strong>: AI for visual understanding of health-related signals and activities.
- <strong>Approach</strong>: Robust computer vision and multi‑modal learning with privacy-aware design.
- <strong>Impact</strong>: Support clinicians and individuals with actionable, explainable insights.

### Frequently Asked Questions (subset)

<details>
  <summary><strong>What is the aim of SeeVita?</strong></summary>
  SeeVita aims to translate cutting‑edge computer vision and AI into practical tools that can help assess activities and wellbeing in a privacy‑conscious manner.
  </details>

<details>
  <summary><strong>How does SeeVita handle privacy?</strong></summary>
  We prioritize privacy by leveraging secure processing pipelines, de‑identification where feasible, and best practices for data governance.
  </details>
