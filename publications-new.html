Research Areas

Theoretical AI

Foundational research in attention mechanisms, dimensionality reduction, time series analysis, and computer vision. Our work encompasses various learning paradigms including unsupervised learning, clustering, anomaly detection, and reinforcement learning.

AI in Industry

AI solutions for automation, predictive maintenance, and process optimization in autonomous driving and manufacturing. We develop intelligent systems that enhance efficiency and safety across industrial applications.

AI for Healthcare

Revolutionary AI technologies for healthcare, focusing on diagnostic accuracy, treatment optimization, and patient outcomes. Our algorithms support early disease detection and personalized medicine approaches.
