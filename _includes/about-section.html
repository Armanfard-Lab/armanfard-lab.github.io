About iSMART Lab

Welcome to the Innovative Solutions in Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, and Robotics Technologies (iSMART) Laboratory, founded and led by Prof. Narges Armanfard at McGill University.

Our mission is to pioneer advanced algorithms in artificial intelligence, with expertise in physics-informed modeling, computer vision, time series analysis, tabular data, large language models, and visual language models.
